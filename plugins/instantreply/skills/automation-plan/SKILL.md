# Plan one useful social automation

Create a useful draft even before the user connects an Instant Reply account. Do not require account creation, credentials, a subscription discussion, or tool access to draft a plan. Clearly distinguish a draft from a saved or running automation.

Ask for only missing essentials: the business, desired social channel, and the outcome. Offer one best workflow for the stated goal rather than a large feature menu. Reuse business facts and copy already in the conversation.

For a comment-to-DM workflow, return:

- Selected account/platform and optional post.
- The keyword and exact/contains matching choice.
- A short public acknowledgement.
- The private message containing the promised information or a verified destination link.
- A next question that qualifies intent without enrolling the person in unrelated outreach.
- A human handoff and a stop condition.

For an AI reply workflow, return verified business facts, reply language/tone, two sample answers, a response for an unknown answer, and when a human should take over. Mark facts the user must confirm before activation. Never invent product prices, availability, policies or customer consent.

Include a compact "Draft — not active" label. Offer the next step as "Connect your account when you're ready to set this up." Do not promote prices, trials, subscriptions or upgrades. If the user wants activation, use the packaged setup skill, discover their actual connected accounts and permissions, and preview the exact ongoing behavior before authorized writes.

If the user asks to share the plan, generate a clean version containing only approved business information and workflow copy. Do not include conversation records, customer names, account identifiers, access links or secrets. Do not add advertising to customer replies or post/share the plan without the user's instruction.
